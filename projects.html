AI Model Poisoning & Backdoor Detection Scanner

An open-source scanner that detects data poisoning and backdoor triggers in ML classification pipelines, combining spectral signature analysis and activation clustering. Validated against public healthcare-imaging benchmarks and one non-healthcare critical-infrastructure-relevant dataset, with results reported against MITRE ATLAS technique IDs and the NIST AI RMF "Measure" function.
